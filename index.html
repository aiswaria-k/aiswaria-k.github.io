About Me

I am an ocean engineering researcher with a background in civil engineering and offshore structures. My doctoral research at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay investigated wave energy enhancement using a point absorber integrated with a chambered breakwater. My broader interests include marine renewable energy, wave–structure interaction and the hydrodynamics of offshore systems.

My current research also engages with upper-ocean mixing, transport processes and biogeochemical dynamics. I am interested in combining rigorous engineering analysis with collaborative research to address challenges in ocean and offshore environments.

Research Interests

Marine Renewable Energy

Wave energy converters, point absorbers, hybrid wave-energy systems and offshore renewable energy.

Offshore Structures

Marine hydrodynamics, wave–structure interaction, floating systems and structural response in waves.

Experimental and Numerical Modelling

Physical model experiments, motion tracking, computational fluid dynamics and hydrodynamic analysis.

Ocean Dynamics

Upper-ocean turbulence, transport and mixing, and their influence on biogeochemical processes.
